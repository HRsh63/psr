What It Detects

🎭

Lip-Sync Consistency

Analyzes frame-level mouth movement vs. audio waveform alignment to flag AI-dubbed or face-swapped content.

🔊

Audio Splicing Detection

Scans for spectral discontinuities, sudden silence transitions, and energy jumps that reveal cut-and-paste audio edits.

🗜️

Recompression Artifacts

Identifies double-compression signatures and block-level JPEG/H.264 artifacts that appear when videos are re-exported after editing.

📊

Forensic Risk Score

Aggregates all signals into a single manipulation confidence score with a LOW / SUSPICIOUS / HIGH verdict badge.
